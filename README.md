# OUT OF PLACE: Playtest 0.5 (browser version)

**Play:** open the game page, then click or tap **BEGIN MISSION**.

OUT OF PLACE is a non-violent third-person stealth game: sneak in, take what you came for, and
get out unseen. This is an early **prototype**, so expect placeholder graphics and rough edges.
It has four missions: The Facility, Archive Complex, Blackout and Shapeshifter.

## Before you play

- **Computer:** a desktop browser (Chrome, Edge or Firefox) with a keyboard and mouse. Click in
  the game to capture the mouse; pressing **Esc** frees the mouse and pauses the game.
- **Phone or tablet:** hold it **sideways**. On-screen controls appear by themselves (see below).
  Touch controls are new, so please tell us how they feel.
- **The first load takes a moment:** the game is about 40 MB.
- The browser version looks a little simpler than the downloadable one, and may run slower. The
  pause menu shows the frame rate (FPS); please include it in your feedback.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard / mouse | Touch |
|---|---|---|
| Move | W A S D | Thumb on the left side (push further to go faster) |
| Look | Mouse | Drag anywhere else |
| Run (loud) | Shift | **RUN** (tap on/off) |
| Crouch (quiet) | C | **C** |
| Interact / shapeshift (hold) | E | **E** (hold) |
| Aim and throw a distraction | Hold G, release to throw | Hold **G**, drag to aim, release to throw |
| Map | Tab | **MAP** |
| Pause, settings and missions | Esc | **II** |

- **Start any mission:** pause → **Play Mission 01–04**.
- **Restart:** pause → **Restart mission**. After you succeed or fail: **R**, or **RESTART** on a
  touch screen.

## The playtest log

The game keeps a small text log of each attempt **in your browser only**; nothing is sent
anywhere. To send it back: pause, then **Download Playtest Log**, then attach
`playtest_log.txt` to your reply along with your answers below.

5. Any moment that felt confusing, frustrating, or especially good?
